Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page explains the privacy practices for visitors to the Fakirchand Vidyapeeth Educational Institute website.

Information you provide

The website currently displays an admission enquiry form, but it does not connect to a server or submit the form data. Do not enter sensitive personal information in that form. If you contact the institute by telephone or an external messaging service, the information you choose to share is handled through that service and by the recipient.

Cookies and third-party services

This website may load fonts and images from third-party services. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies apply.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to explain advertising cookies, personalised or non-personalised ads, and available privacy controls before or when those services are used.

How to contact us

For website or privacy questions, call the contact number displayed on the Contact page. The institute's exact postal address and official email should be confirmed with the administration before being published here.
